Hazard Advisories & Safety Guidance

Official contingency guidelines developed by disaster management authorities to protect families and emergency responders.

Cyclone Preparedness

Tropical storms and hurricane-velocity coastal winds

High Risk
Warning Signs & Early Indicators
  • Rapid barometric pressure drop followed by abnormal sea swell.
  • Dense low cloud formation and sudden sustained wind velocity surges.
  • Official meteorological cyclone watch status upgrades.
Immediate Survival Actions
  • Remain indoors away from glass windows and open verandas.
  • Switch off electrical main breaker and domestic gas lines.
  • Evacuate low-lying shoreline habitations when instructed by authorities.
Community Coordination
  • Check on elderly and medically vulnerable neighbors.
  • Only share official verified disaster broadcast bulletins.
  • Adhere to designated regional evacuation corridors.
Emergency Helplines
  • 1078 - National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA)
  • 112 - Emergency Response Support System (ERSS)

Flash Flood & Surge Safety

Inundation, storm surges, and river breach protocol

High Risk
Warning Signs & Inundation Triggers
  • Persistent extreme precipitation exceeding drainage capacity.
  • Rapid rise of backwaters, river channels, and municipal canals.
  • Sustained high tide coinciding with heavy upstream outflow.
Immediate Evacuation Protocol
  • Never attempt to walk or drive through moving water currents.
  • Move immediately to multi-story structures or elevated ground.
  • Disconnect electrical appliances to prevent electrocution hazards.
Health & Water Safety
  • Do not consume unboiled tap or ground water during flood situations.
  • Beware of snakes and wildlife seeking higher ground.
  • Keep water purification tablets ready in emergency supplies.
Emergency Helplines
  • 1070 - State Disaster Relief Commissioner
  • 108 - Emergency Medical & Ambulance Services

Landslide & Slope Stabilization

Debris flows, rockfalls, and saturated slope shifts

Medium Risk
Warning Signs & Terrain Movement
  • New fissures or widening cracks in foundations, slopes, or roads.
  • Trees, fences, or utility poles tilting noticeably downhill.
  • Faint rumbling sounds that gradually increase in intensity.
Immediate Protective Actions
  • Evacuate rapidly out of the direct downslope slide path.
  • If escape is obstructed, curl tightly and protect your head.
  • Stay awake and alert during prolonged heavy nocturnal storms.
Slope Management Tips
  • Avoid cutting into steep slope toes for unauthorized construction.
  • Ensure hillside drainage channels remain clear of debris.
Emergency Helplines
  • 112 - Emergency Response
  • 101 - Fire and Rescue Department

Marine Chemical & Oil Spill

Hazardous maritime discharge and shoreline contamination

Low Risk
Warning Signs & Contamination Clues
  • Iridescent chemical slicks or pungent chemical odors on coastlines.
  • Unusual behavioral distress or mortality among marine fauna.
  • Oily tar balls depositing along beaches and port docks.
Immediate Precautionary Measures
  • Stay upwind from vapors to avoid toxic inhalation.
  • Do not handle or salvage unknown barrels or chemical containers.
  • Report coordinate sightings immediately to Coast Guard channels.
Public Health Guidance
  • Avoid swimming or fishing in contaminated shoreline zones.
  • Adhere to official advisories on local seafood consumption.
Emergency Helplines
  • 1554 - Indian Coast Guard Search & Pollution Rescue
  • 112 - Hazmat Emergency Unit
